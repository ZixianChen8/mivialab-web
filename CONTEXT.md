# MiviaLab Website

Vocabulary for the MiviaLab marketing site — what things are called, not how they are built.

## Language

**Lean multi-page**:
A small set of real URLs where the homepage does the persuasion work and additional pages exist only when content earns its own address.
_Avoid_: Single-page site, classic agency sitemap, multi-page brochure site

**Primary contact action**:
The one main way a stranger reaches out — a short contact form — with WeChat QR as a strong secondary for community leads.
_Avoid_: Contact menu, omnichannel contact, equal social links

**Landing page**:
The homepage (`/`) — the main persuasion surface. Thin topics that are not ready for their own URL live here as sections, not as separate pages.
_Avoid_: Brochure home, marketing home (when used to mean something else)

**Work detail**:
A dedicated URL (`/work/[slug]`) for a project that has enough substance to stand alone (story, visuals, outcomes). Not every portfolio mention earns one.
_Avoid_: Case study page, project page, portfolio page (as synonyms for any work mention)

**Landing sections** (canonical order on `/`):
Hero → Outcomes → Work → Offer → About → Contact. Team and Blog are out of v1. Proof is added only when real proof assets exist.
_Avoid_: Services page content as a separate top-level nav item; Team placeholders; empty Proof slots

**Outcomes**:
Buyer-facing results (speed, security, custom build, full service) — not engineering jargon.
_Avoid_: Features, differentiators (as visitor-facing labels), value props

**Offer**:
What MiviaLab sells on the landing page: the site build plus the monthly care plan, without a fabricated price while the monthly amount is still open.
_Avoid_: Services (as a page), Pricing (as a page), packages grid

**Work** (section):
The landing-page portfolio strip showing the real client and clearly labeled concept samples, with links to Work details when they exist.
_Avoid_: Portfolio (as the section name), Our clients

**About**:
A short studio note on the landing page, including bilingual capability (EN / FR / 中文) as a premium feature — not the brand identity.
_Avoid_: Team page, Our story, About us page

**Contact** (section):
The landing-page end of the funnel: short form as primary contact action, WeChat QR as strong secondary, other channels present but subordinate.
_Avoid_: Contact page (for v1), Get in touch hub

**Real client work**:
A shipped project for an actual client. In v1 this is Meng Wei Yue Opera Studio. Real client work may earn a Work detail URL.
_Avoid_: Case study (when it blurs real vs concept), portfolio piece (ambiguous)

**Concept sample**:
A labeled sample or concept site shown to demonstrate capability — never implied to be a real client. Lives on the landing Work section in v1; does not get a Work detail URL until it has enough substance, and stays labeled as a concept even then.
_Avoid_: Fake client, demo client, sample client, fictional case study

**Site language (v1)**:
The marketing site ships in English only. Bilingual capability (EN / FR / 中文) is something MiviaLab offers to clients, not a requirement that this site itself is translated in v1.
_Avoid_: Localized marketing site, trilingual site, language switcher (for v1)

**Work detail sections** (canonical order):
Hero (project + real-client framing) → Story → Visuals → Outcomes (facts only) → CTA into the primary contact action on the landing page.
_Avoid_: Related work carousel, fake metrics, separate contact funnel on Work details
