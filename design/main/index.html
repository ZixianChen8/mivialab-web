About

A Canadian studio in the Ottawa–Toronto corridor

MiviaLab is a web design and development studio for small businesses. English-first, premium, and straightforward — we build sites that look professional and stay easy for owners because we handle them end to end.

We sit between a freelance site builder and a big agency: the structure, SEO setup, and ongoing care of a studio, with accessible pricing, direct communication, and a scope that flexes to your business. When a project needs it, we deliver bilingual sites in English, French, and 中文 — a premium capability, not our brand identity.

  • Accessible pricing built for small-business budgets
  • Direct communication — you talk to the people building your site
  • Flexible scope that grows with your business
  • Full service, so you never have to touch the site

How we work

A clear path from idea to launch

No jargon and no guesswork — you always know what happens next, and we stay on after launch instead of disappearing.

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    We learn your business, services, customers, and goals — and look at any site you have now.

  2. 02

    Plan & scope

    We map pages, structure, and contact pathways, then agree on deliverables, timeline, and what's included.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    We design the layout and build it in custom code — mobile included, with basic SEO set up from the start.

  4. 04

    Review & launch

    You review the content and details; we test across devices, links, and forms, then take it live.

  5. 05

    Ongoing care

    Maintenance, SEO, updates, and marketing support keep the site healthy as your business grows.
