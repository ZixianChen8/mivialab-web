# Cartoon Motion

Playful logo-first hero that opens into a premium, cinematic story layout.

## Open

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser (no build step).

## Mood

- **Hero:** soft sky-to-peach gradient, doodles, orbit chips, centered transparent logo with mouse-reactive 3D tilt
- **Below the fold:** cinematic chapter rhythm — numbered sections, media-forward work grid, tour-style process list, dark process/care bands, centered contact
- Fredoka + Nunito throughout (cartoon voice, premium spacing)
- Chained scroll storytelling: staggered chapter reveals, blur→sharp entrances, story progress rail
