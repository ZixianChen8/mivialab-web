Services & expertise

Custom web design & development

Hand-built code — not Wix or Squarespace templates. Every site is tailored to the business, optimized for speed, and backed by ongoing security and care.

  1. 01

    Speed that keeps customers

    Sites that load fast so you stop losing visitors. We build lean and prove it with PageSpeed Insights — not marketing claims.

  2. 02

    Security you can trust

    Protect customer data with regular reviews and updates. Your site stays current without you chasing plugins.

  3. 03

    Fully custom build

    Clean, editable code shaped around your business — not a bloated template you fight every time you need a change.

  4. 04

    Full service after launch

    Monthly care covers maintenance, security, ongoing customization, and continuous SEO. You run the business; we run the site.

  5. 05

    Bilingual when it matters

    EN / FR / 中文 delivery as a premium capability — including genuine French for Ottawa’s bilingual reality.

Edits to existing content are included in the monthly plan. New features, pages, or integrations after delivery are quoted separately.
