# MiviaLab — Strategy & Positioning Decisions

## Company

**MiviaLab** — a web development and digital design studio that builds modern, fast, custom websites for small businesses, with supporting branding/graphic design (and possibly digital marketing later).

- **Mission:** Help small businesses succeed online with websites that are modern, professional, easy to use, and built around real business needs.
- **Vision:** Become a trusted web development partner for small businesses through high-quality design and a straightforward development process.
- **Based in:** Ottawa–Toronto corridor.

## 1. The website's single job

Get strangers to trust MiviaLab enough to reach out. The entire site funnels toward one contact action.

- **Primary CTA:** one low-friction action (recommended: a short contact form, with a WeChat QR as a strong secondary for community leads).
- **Secondary contact:** email, phone, Instagram, LinkedIn, RedNote, WeChat — present but smaller and below the primary action. Six options is a menu, not a funnel; keep the hierarchy disciplined.

## 2. Positioning — the key decision

Public brand = a Canadian / Ottawa web design & development studio. English-first, premium, broad. Not labeled as a "Chinese niche" studio.

But: the Chinese community network is the go-to-market wedge — the way the first ~10 clients get landed (language, WeChat/RedNote, the Opera Studio client). A wedge is the door you walk through, not the room you live in.

The reconciliation we adopted:

- **Public face:** "MiviaLab — web design & development studio in Ottawa." Clean, English-first, broad. No "Chinese" in the headline.
- **Bilingual capability shown as a premium feature, not an identity:** "Fully bilingual sites (EN / FR / 中文)" — which also satisfies the real Ottawa EN/FR expectation.
- **WeChat / RedNote** present as options, not the whole contact strategy.
- **First clients & sample sites** sourced through the community wedge, while a non-Chinese visitor still sees a clean Canadian studio.

## 3. Differentiators (translated into buyer outcomes)

Lead with outcomes the owner can feel/verify, not engineering jargon:

| Capability | Buyer outcome |
|---|---|
| Speed / no bloat | "Loads fast so you stop losing customers" — provable via PageSpeed Insights scores/screenshots on the site. |
| Security | Prevent customer data loss, ensure transaction security, regular security reviews & updates. |
| Full custom build (no Wix/Squarespace) | Clean, fully customizable code vs. bloated template platforms. |
| Full service | The owner never has to touch their site; MiviaLab handles it. |

## 4. Full-service / pricing model

- Monthly subscription covers ongoing maintenance, security updates, continuous customization, and continuous SEO.
- **Included vs. extra rule:** edits to existing content/upkeep are included; adding new features/pages/integrations after delivery is quoted separately, even for subscribers.

> **⚠️ Open items to nail down:** a clear(er) line for what counts as "new feature = extra," and a ballpark monthly price to anchor buyers. (Still undefined — flagged as a risk to resolve.)

## 5. Languages

- **Default:** English.
- **Options:** EN / FR / 中文.
- French is genuinely deliverable (founder is fluent), which reinforces the legitimate Ottawa/Canadian bilingual positioning.

## 6. Target verticals (for first sample sites & outreach)

No "everyone." Recommended starting set (pick two):

1. Restaurants / bubble tea / bakeries
2. TCM clinics / acupuncture / massage / dental
3. Beauty: nail / hair / lash / med-spa
4. Tutoring / education / music & arts studios (matches the real client — recommended)
5. Immigration / accounting / real estate (high deal value)

**Suggested combo:** #4 (education/arts) + one high-money vertical (#2 or #5). Final two: still to be chosen.
