## 1. Company Overview

MiviaLab is a digital studio that helps small businesses, local organizations, professional service providers, and growing teams build a clear and credible online presence.

The studio's primary focus is website planning, website design and development, basic search engine optimization, conversion-focused website structure, ongoing website maintenance, and supporting promotional materials.

MiviaLab is especially suitable for businesses that:

* Do not currently have a website
* Have an outdated or difficult-to-use website
* Depend mainly on social media, referrals, or Google Maps
* Need a more professional online presence
* Want customers to understand their services more easily
* Need a website that supports inquiries, bookings, calls, or other customer actions
* Need a local branch website connected to a larger company or head office
* Need ongoing website updates but do not have an internal website team
* Are preparing to run Google Ads and need a suitable website or landing page
* Need affordable promotional materials for events, services, or social media

MiviaLab's role is not only to make a website visually attractive. Its role is to organize business information, improve credibility, create a clearer customer journey, and build a website that supports real business communication and customer inquiries.

## 2. MiviaLab's Core Positioning

MiviaLab can be positioned as:

* A large digital marketing agency
* A full-service Google Ads agency

Compared with a large agency, MiviaLab offers:

# 4. Search Engine Optimization Services

## 4.1 Basic SEO Setup

MiviaLab can provide basic SEO setup during website development.

SEO, or search engine optimization, means improving the website's content, structure, and technical settings so search engines such as Google can understand the website more accurately.

Basic SEO setup may include:

* Page title setup
* Google search result description setup
* Heading structure
* Clear page URLs
* Image descriptions
* Internal page links
* Website sitemap setup
* Search engine access checks
* Mobile display checks
* Page loading checks
* Basic keyword placement
* Local service information
* Contact information consistency
* Indexing preparation

For example, a website for an Ottawa-based business may include clear information about:

* The services offered
* The geographic area served
* The business address
* The office phone number
* The local team
* Nearby communities served

This helps Google understand the relationship between the business, its services, and its location.

### Important limitation

MiviaLab does not guarantee:

* A first-page Google ranking
* A number-one ranking
* Immediate search traffic
* A specific number of leads
* Rankings for highly competitive keywords

SEO results depend on many factors, including competition, website history, content quality, reputation, external links, industry conditions, and continued investment.
