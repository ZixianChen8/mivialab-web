# About brand story scroll

**Status:** Graduated. The former sandbox `/about-2` is now production `/about`. The sandbox route is removed.

The studio's brand story (origin, AI-slop problem, thoughtful-AI stance, lower-price promise) deserves a scroll narrative stronger than static paragraphs, and licensed Made with GSAP packs are available locally under `assets/gsap/`. The page tells the approved two-paragraph brand story as one continuous read split across six motion sections, each sentence appearing exactly once: **mwg_022** (pinned stage, three side-by-side columns that swap word-by-word) cold-opens under the nav with paragraph one (origin, problem, slop detail); **mwg_011** rides the thesis line "The real question is how you use it." horizontally with elastic letters, its header carrying "AI isn't going away..."; **mwg_102** flips the mindless-vs-thoughtful contrast in 3D between two center-stage lines; **mwg_097** follows as a centered scroll essay with the founding idea and the required line that we use AI strategically to cut down the time cost of the technical work and pass those savings on through lower prices, plus the human-judgment result; **mwg_098** types the closing line "Never a mass-produced template..." along rotating concentric arcs on a pinned stage. The **founder spotlight** takes only the cursor-follow card mechanic from **mwg_039**: Michael and Olivia share one viewport with left/right-biased name, role, and bio (no card chrome on the text). One shared floating portrait trails the pointer across both bands and swaps photo when the cursor enters that founder's band. The whole page runs a ~1.5x type scale (the 150 percent zoom feel the founders preferred). On touch and reduced motion the follower is dropped and each band shows its own static portrait (gentle float on touch only); with reduced motion every pinned story section also renders as stacked static text. Rejected early on: a single-preset spine (022-only or 097-only cut too much of the story), mwg_009 sentence spine (loses too much copy), mwg_039's background-collage text (hurts readability), scroll-driven founder swap (visitors should read each person fully), and a scroll-driven card drift on touch (swap is the point, not the drift). Tunables live in `web/lib/story-anim-config.ts`. Implementation: `web/app/about/`.
