How should we rebuild the fashion-editorial page?

All three fill images + MiviaLab copy. They differ in how sections connect.

A

Enrich in place

Keep the current 7 sections. Swap in picsum placeholders, thicken MiviaLab copy, add captions/pull-quotes between sections, and tighten spacing so slides feel less isolated.

Best if: you like the structure and just want it less empty.

B

Editorial magazine flow Recommended

Keep the fashion look, but recompose as a continuous magazine: full-bleed image breaks, overlapping text/image, a sticky story spine (Studio → Services → Work → Contact), and denser about/work spreads.

Best if: you want it to feel like one scroll story, not stacked slides.

C

Collapse & intensify

Cut to 4–5 denser sections (Hero, Studio story, Work, Contact). Drop the circular statement and petal testimonials until real proof exists. Every remaining section is image-heavy and copy-complete.

Best if: emptiness comes from too many thin sections.

Tradeoffs at a glance

A — Enrich

  • Fastest
  • Preserves existing interactions

Risk

  • May still feel slide-like

B — Magazine

  • Strongest continuous flow
  • Still fashion-editorial

Risk

  • More CSS/layout work

C — Collapse

  • Least empty by force
  • Clearer funnel

Risk

  • Loses distinctive editorial moments
